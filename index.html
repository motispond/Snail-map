Small ceramic snails from Mot, and where they went. About ↗ Snail Map is part of Mot, a ceramics project started in 2025 by the Korean creator Momo Hwang, making snails out of clay and finding them new homes. This site was built to count how far these small ceramic snails have travelled from Mot, and to keep a record of each goodbye and each arrival. Contact ↗
